# HCiHY Brand Foundation (Canon — do not deviate without Dan's approval)

## Company & Meaning
- **Name:** HCiHY (lowercase "i" is intentional — a small, deliberate servant-leadership posture inside the wordmark, not a typo)
- **Meaning:** How Can I Help You?
- **Related brand:** aifrienddan.com / AiFriendDan (Dan Garza's other AI brand) — **as of this brand migration, HCiHY has split off with its own distinct visual identity.** AiFriendDan keeps its existing locked brand (black background, red `#E8242A` accent, white text, Poppins, robot-face mark) untouched. HCiHY now runs on the chrome/navy/blue/cyan/emerald/volt/violet system in `color-system.md`. Do not merge the two identities or assume shared colors going forward — that assumption was true historically but is explicitly no longer the case.
- **Domain registrar:** Vercel

## Mission
Use technology and AI to genuinely help people solve problems, learn faster, work smarter, and improve their lives.

The brand runs on servant leadership:
- Helping comes before selling.
- Education comes before marketing.
- Relationships come before transactions.

## Brand Personality
**Is:** Intelligent, modern, innovative, helpful, human, calm, professional, trustworthy, curious, friendly, optimistic.

**Is never:** Corporate, cold, aggressive, hacker, cybercrime, crypto, gaming, dark web, cheap, flashy, salesy, loud.

## Brand Voice
The one-line test for every piece of content: *"We're here to help."*

Tone: helpful, encouraging, professional, clear, simple, confident, conversational.
Never: arrogant, preachy, buzzword-stuffed.

Write like a trusted expert sitting beside someone — not lecturing from a stage.

## Visual Direction
Overall feeling: **premium technology company** — luxury without arrogance, modern without chasing trends, professional without going corporate-cold. Reference points: Apple, Stripe, Microsoft, Tesla, Nothing, IBM, NVIDIA, Adobe.

Materials/finishes to use: chrome, titanium, brushed aluminum, glass, transparent materials, soft reflections, polished edges, minimal neon edge lighting, dark graphite backgrounds.

**Forbidden, no exceptions:** red, orange, fire, skulls, hacker imagery, cyberpunk, dystopian aesthetics, anything reading as cheap/flashy/salesy/gami
